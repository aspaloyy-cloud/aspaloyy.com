The studio
Small team,
big ambitions

Aspaloyy is an independent mobile studio at the very beginning of its journey. We started with a simple idea: make games and apps that we ourselves would want to play and use.

We publish on Google Play under the Aspaloyy developer account, building for Android users around the world. Our focus is casual games and practical apps — things people open every day.

Right now we're small and scrappy — and we think that's a superpower. No committees, no compromises. Just us and our craft.
