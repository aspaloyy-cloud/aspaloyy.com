How I work

I am not a classically trained programmer and I do not claim a formal degree in development. What I do is take a goal and drive it to a finished result: break it into tasks, research what each task actually requires, build the pipeline, and use modern tooling as my working environment. I learn the thing and build the thing in the same motion — and I finish.
